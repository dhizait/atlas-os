### *LOCKING THE ATLAS STRATEGY - "ONE STOP SHOP" PRINCIPLES*

If the goal is _pick the best from the best_ and be the _one stop shop_, then we need to cement 3 pillars into every stream:

#### *1. EXTENSIBILITY*
*The Atlas Way*: Everything is a plugin/module  
- *Greenfield*: App store model. 3rd parties can build "Atlas Loans", "Atlas Treasury" on our APIs
- *Brownfield*: Connector SDK. New core in 2 weeks, not 6 months
- *T24 Core*: Business logic as services. Swap AA module without touching Deposits
Rule: `No hardcoding. Everything configurable via JSON/YAML`

#### *2. FUTURE-PROOF ARCHITECTURE*
Build once, run anywhere for 15 years
- *Cloud Native*: Kubernetes, Event-driven, Stateless services
- *AI Ready*: Every transaction emits an event → feeds AI for fraud, credit scoring, ESG
- *Composable*: Bank can buy Greenfield Onboarding + Brownfield GL + T24 Core AA. Mix & Match
- *Standards*: ISO20022, BIAN, Open Banking APIs baked in Day 1

#### *3. ROOM FOR INNOVATION*
*The Atlas Lab* inside the platform
- *Sandbox*: Banks + Fintechs can test new products on Atlas without touching prod
- *Partner IP*: Like you said with Temenos. We build the novel T24 integration, they sell it. We take rev share
- *ESG + Impact*: Auto reporting like we did for GRIT. This becomes our differentiator vs T24
