### *3. THE 3 LAWS OF DRIVERS*

This is what keeps us safe:

1.  *Law 1: Event Only*  
    Driver never does `db.query()`. It subscribes to `AccountCreditedEvent` and acts.
2.  *Law 2: Hot Swap*  
    `docker compose down iso_driver && docker compose up iso_driver_v2`. Kernel stays up.
3.  *Law 3: Toggle Off*  
    BATCH-06 can kill MNO Driver for 1 tenant. Kernel + Accounts still run.
