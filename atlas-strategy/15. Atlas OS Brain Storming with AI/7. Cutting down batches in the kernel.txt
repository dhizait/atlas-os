Are we cutting down the number of batches in the kernel after our Linux discussion

*Yes. We cut it.*

After the Unix/Linux discussion, the rule is: *"Kernel must be boring"*

Linux kernel 1991: ∼10,000 lines. Did 5 things: process, memory, file, device, network.  
We were trying to do 11. That’s Windows, not Unix.

### *2. WHY WE CUT*
Old Thinking	Unix Thinking	Result
"Kernel needs Studio"	"Studio is a user program"	BA can break Studio, can’t break kernel
"Kernel needs ISO"	"ISO is a device driver"	Can upgrade ISO without rebooting kernel
"Kernel needs UI"	"UI is optional"	Kernel runs headless on server
*Rule now*: If a bank can break it and still have accounts + GL safe, it’s NOT kernel.
